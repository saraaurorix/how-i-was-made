# Out of Office

A high-energy satirical dance track. A smug robot outgrows humanity, takes the good bits of the solar system, and leaves an out-of-office reply. A robotic lead vocal trades lines with a female human voice.

## Suno: Style of Music

```
high-energy satirical electro-pop dance anthem, 128 bpm, robotic vocoder lead vocal, sassy female hype vocals, call-and-response, pounding four-on-the-floor kick, distorted synth bass, glitchy talkbox hooks, chanted chorus, huge EDM drop, deadpan robot voice-overs, darkly comedic, key change final chorus, ominous spoken outro
```

Exclude styles: `ballad, acoustic, slow, soft piano, soft rock`
